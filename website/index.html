Your music on Discord, line by line.

Vybecord puts whatever is playing on your PC on your Discord profile, with the lyric being sung right now. Spotify, YouTube, Apple Music, Twitch and most other players on Windows. Install it, press play, and that's it.

Features

Everything your profile was missing

One small app in your tray. It finds the song, finds the words, and keeps your Discord card in step with both.

The line being sung, on your card

Your status shows the current lyric with the next one under it, timed to the millisecond and corrected for Discord's own delay. Mark a wrong match and it never comes back; nudge the timing and it's remembered for that song.

01:02.40Every street we ever walked at three
01:06.90Say it once more before the signal drops
01:11.30The city hums the rest of it for us

Up to five presences

Spotify on one card, a YouTube video on the next, a Twitch stream on a third. Each has its own lyrics switch and can be pinned to its own player.

Big lyrics

One click fills the window with the words, large, on the colour of the cover. Press Esc to come back.

The real name on the card

Your card says Spotify, YouTube, SoundCloud, Apple Music, Twitch or Kick, not the name of some app. Pick the activity type, what the status line shows and a button of your own.

  • Listening
  • Watching
  • Playing
  • Competing

Translated and romanised

Translate lyrics into 16 languages, in the window and on Discord if you like. Romanisation covers Japanese with kanji, Korean, Chinese, Cyrillic and more.

夜明けまで走ろう yoake made hashirou Let's run until dawn

Your listening, charted

Time listened each day, your top artists and tracks, over a week, a month, a year or all time. Optional Last.fm scrobbling, with your own Last.fm API key.

No ads on your status

During an ad break, Spotify swaps the song for the advertiser. Vybecord spots it and keeps your card clear until the music is back, while sparing interludes from the album you're playing.

WithoutZephyr Energy, 50% off this week
WithNothing shown until the music is back

Stays out of your way

  • Lives in the tray. Closing the window keeps your status up.
  • Presence and Lyrics switches on Now playing and in the tray menu.
  • Steps aside when you're away, back on the first key press.
  • Updates itself, installed when you quit.
Setup

Ready in about a minute

Nothing to configure and nothing to sign in to. The app's Help & setup page ticks itself off as you go.

  1. Download the installer

    One file, the latest version, straight from the project's GitHub releases.

    Download

  2. Run it

    Windows may say it protected your PC, because the installer isn't code-signed. Choose More info, then Run anyway.

  3. Press play

    Open the Discord desktop app and play something, anywhere. Your card updates on its own.

Needs Windows 10 (version 1809 or later) or Windows 11, 64-bit, and the Discord desktop app. In Discord, activity sharing has to be on, under User Settings → Activity Privacy.

Optional add-ons

Two ways to make it sharper

Vybecord works on its own from the first song. These fill in what Windows can't tell it.

Spicetify extension

For the Spotify desktop app
  • Spotify's own synced lyrics, when the song has them.
  • Song changes the moment they happen, with the exact position.
  • Every artist, the playlist you're in, and Spotify's cover art.

Install Spicetify first. Then Set up automatically, under Settings → Integrations → Spotify, does the rest; Spotify restarts once.

Browser extension

Chrome, Edge, Brave, Opera, Vivaldi and Firefox
  • Tells Spotify Web, YouTube, YouTube Music, SoundCloud, Bandcamp, Twitch and Kick apart.
  • The real link to the track, so friends can open it.
  • The exact position from the page, and live-stream uptime.
It comes with the app: Settings → Integrations → Browser extension opens its folder and copies the address to paste. Or download it here.
Chrome, Edge and others Firefox
How to install it by hand
  1. Unzip the file somewhere it can stay.
  2. Open chrome://extensions (edge://extensions in Edge) and turn on Developer mode.
  3. Click Load unpacked and pick the unzipped folder.
  4. In Firefox 121 or later, open about:debugging, then This Firefox → Load Temporary Add-on, and pick the zip. Firefox removes it when it closes.
Privacy

Your listening stays yours

Vybecord talks to Discord through the Discord app on your own PC. The one exception is cover art that exists only in your own files: it can be published so Discord can show it, and that has a switch.

No account

Nothing to sign up for, and it never asks for your Discord login.

No telemetry

No analytics and no tracking. A problem report goes out only when you send one.

Kept on your PC

Your history, your lyrics and your settings live in your own data folder.

Only what's needed

Lookups send the song's title and artist to find lyrics and covers. Nothing about you.

FAQ

Questions

Is Vybecord free?

Yes. It's free and open source under the MIT licence, with no paid tier and no ads.

Why does Windows warn me about the installer?

The installer isn't signed with a paid code-signing certificate, so SmartScreen doesn't recognise it yet. Choose More info, then Run anyway.

To make sure you have the genuine file, compare its SHA-256 with the one shown under the download button and on the release page.

My status isn't showing on Discord

Vybecord needs the Discord desktop app running on the same PC; Discord in a browser can't show it. Then check that activity sharing is on, under User Settings → Activity Privacy.

The bottom of Vybecord's sidebar says whether Discord is connected and, if your status is hidden, why.

Which players does it work with?

Most of what shows up in the Windows volume overlay: Spotify, Apple Music, Deezer, Tidal, Amazon Music, VLC, foobar2000, MusicBee, AIMP, Winamp, MediaMonkey, and music or video playing in Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Brave, Opera, Vivaldi or Zen.

Not Windows Media Player, Groove Music, Movies & TV or the SoundCloud app from the Microsoft Store. For SoundCloud, play it in a browser with the extension instead.

The browser extension tells YouTube, SoundCloud, Twitch and the other sites apart.

Is it allowed by Discord?

Vybecord uses Rich Presence, the feature Discord made for apps and games to show what you're doing, through the connection the Discord app opens on your PC. It never asks for your login or your token and doesn't modify Discord.

Is there a Mac or Linux version?

Not at the moment. Vybecord reads the Windows media session, which only exists on Windows 10 (version 1809) and later.

How do updates work?

Vybecord checks for a new version on its own and downloads it in the background. It installs when you quit the app, or right away from the banner in the window or the tray menu.

How do I uninstall it?

From Windows Settings → Apps, like any other app. Your settings and history stay in %APPDATA%\Vybecord until you delete that folder.
